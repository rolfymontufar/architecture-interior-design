# Furniture
Furniture sizes in cm, W x D x H (mattress: width x length). Clearance = clear use space in front of or around the item. Canonical clearances live in `bedrooms.md` (bed, wardrobe), `living-dining.md` (dining, sofa-table, TV) and `kitchen.md` (aisles). US = converted from inches; EU = European standard.
