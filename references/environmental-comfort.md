## Climate: tropical LatAm vs. Europe
RT (NEUF, CIBSE-AM10; (av) per site):
- **Hot humid tropics:** prioritize cross ventilation and shade over solar gain. Long axis east-west, main façades north and south. Minimize west windows (afternoon sun). Overhangs and shading on all façades; near the equator the sun is nearly overhead, 60-100 cm overhangs shade 120-150 high windows (av). High ceilings (270-300), louvers, high outlets for hot air. Shallow plan (≤ 5 x ceiling height) for cross ventilation.
- **Highland tropics (Bogotá, Quito, CDMX):** temperate; seek moderate solar gain in living areas and night insulation.
- **Europe and temperate Southern Cone:** capture winter sun. Northern hemisphere: living spaces south; Chile, Argentina, Uruguay: north. Overhang sized to block summer sun and admit winter sun. Movable external shading east and west (shutters, blinds). Mediterranean climate: thermal mass and night ventilation.
