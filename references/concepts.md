# Core Concepts
Scope: minimum knowledge to size and evaluate spaces; concept map A1-A19, B1-B9; detail for A3-A8, A16, A17, A19.

## Quick reference
| Topic | Value |
|---|---|
| Net vs. gross | Net ≈ 75-85 % of gross; subtract 15-25 % from listing figures |
| Circulation | 10-15 % of net area, RT (canonical: circulation.md) |
| Program check | Gross ≈ Σ net x (1 + % circulation) x (1 + % walls) |
| Proportion | 1:1.25 to 1:1.5 ideal; 1:2 practical limit; > 1:2 corridor effect (canonical: A8) |
| Min. side | Main furniture + clearances both sides; governs over area |
| Clear dimension | Centerline − ½ wall per side − finishes (3.00 axes, 15 walls → 2.82 clear) |
| Walls (cm) | Drywall 7.3-10; block 12-18; exterior 15-35 |
| Modules | M = 10 cm; kitchen 60; gypsum board 122 x 244 (Americas), 120 x 250-300 (Europe) |
| Scales | 1:100 plans; 1:50 furnished; 1:20-1:25 kitchens and baths |

## A3. Architectural program
List of required spaces with users, activities, furniture, area and adjacencies; basis of any design.
Steps: 1) users (count, ages, mobility, routines); 2) activities per space; 3) furniture and equipment (furniture.md); 4) net area per space = furniture + clearances (min./rec./generous level); 5) sum net areas, add circulation and walls (A6); 6) check against lot and code (coverage/COS, FAR/CUS, setbacks).

| Column | Content | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Space | Name | Master bedroom |
| Zone | Social, private, service | Private |
| Users | Count, profile | 2 adults |
| Activities | What happens | Sleep, dress |
| Furniture | List with sizes | Bed 160x200, 2 nightstands |
| Net area (m²) | Per level | See bedrooms.md |
| Min. side (m) | Minimum clear side | See bedrooms.md |
| Adjacencies | Direct, desirable, avoid | Direct: en-suite |
| Requirements | Light, view, privacy, services | East light |

Check formula: gross ≈ Σ net areas x (1 + % circulation) x (1 + % walls). RT; % in A6.

## A4. Zoning
| Zone | Spaces | Character | Typical location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social | Foyer, living, dining, guest study, terrace, powder room | Public, noise tolerated | Near entrance, ground floor, best view and sun |
| Private | Bedrooms, full baths, walk-in closet, private study | Quiet, isolated | Away from street and entrance; upper floor or own wing |
| Service | Kitchen, pantry, laundry, service yard, staff room, garage, storage | Functional, noise and odors | Next to secondary entrance and garage; least valuable facade |

Rules: kitchen hinges social and service zones; private circulation should not cross the social zone where avoidable; guests should not see bedrooms or laundry from the entrance; group wet areas (A19).

### Orientation (RT, OLG)
| Hemisphere | Facade | Suggested use |
|---|---|---|
| Northern (MX, GT, CO, ES) | South (winter sun) | Living, dining, family room |
| Southern (AR, CL, southern BR) | North (winter sun) | Living, dining, family room |
| Both | East | Bedrooms, breakfast nook (morning sun) |
| Both | West | Avoid bedrooms; garage, storage, baths as thermal buffer; shade with overhangs or screens |
| Both | Pole-facing (N in southern, S in northern) | Service, stairs, study with diffuse light |

Tropics (GT, CO, southern MX): near-overhead sun; priority = shade, cross ventilation, protect west. Sun and ventilation detail: environmental-comfort.md.

## A6. Net vs. gross area
| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Net (usable) area (área útil) | Walkable floor inside walls; excludes walls, columns, shafts | OCU, ARQ |
| Gross area (área construida) | Within outer face of facade walls; includes interior walls and half of party walls (ES criterion) | OCU |
| Gross incl. common areas | Gross + share of common areas (ES) | ARQ |
| Height < 1.50 m | Not counted as net in subsidized housing (ES) (av locally) | ARQ |

| Parameter | Typical value | Note | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net-to-gross gap | 15-25 % | Housing; depends on wall thickness | ARQ |
| Net-to-gross ratio | 0.75-0.85 | RT (inverse of row above) | ARQ |
| Circulation in housing | 10-15 % of net area | RT; > 20 % = inefficient plan; canonical: circulation.md | NEUF (av) |
| Drywall partition | 7.3-10 cm | 48 or 70 mm stud + one 12.5 mm board per side | PLC |
| Block partition | 12-18 cm | Block 10-15 cm + 1.5 cm plaster per side | GCC; RT |
| Exterior masonry wall | 15-35 cm | Warm LatAm ~15-20 cm; Spain with insulation ~25-35 cm | (av) |

Always judge habitability by net area and clear side; codes usually require clear dimensions. Listings give gross area: subtract 15-25 % to estimate net.

## A7. Clear vs. centerline dimension
| Type | Measured | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Clear (face to face) | Between finished wall faces | Habitability, furniture, codes |
| Centerline (axis to axis) | Between wall or column axes | Structure, setting out, grid |
| Nominal | Unit + joint | Modular coordination |

- Formula: clear = centerline − (t₁/2 + t₂/2) − finishes.
- Example: axes 3.00 m, walls 15 cm, plaster 1.5 cm per face: 3.00 − 0.15 − 0.03 = **2.82 m clear**.
- Common mistake: a "3 x 3 m" room drawn to centerlines is 2.82 x 2.82 m clear; a wardrobe or bed may no longer fit.
- Columns and shafts reduce clear width locally: check at the narrowest point.

## A8. Room proportion
| Width:length | Effect | Use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | Static; corners hard to furnish if large | Round-table dining, foyer | RT |
| 1:1.25 to 1:1.5 | Most versatile to furnish | Bedroom, living, dining | RT |
| 1:1.618 (golden) | Classic harmonic ratio | Compositional reference | NEU |
| 1:2 | Practical limit; split into 2 zones | Living-dining, study + lounge | RT |
| > 1:2 | Corridor effect, daylight does not reach back | Only galleries, galley kitchens | RT |

- Min. side governs over area: 9 m² at 2.0 x 4.5 m does not work as a double bedroom.
- Min. side = main furniture + use clearances on both sides (furniture.md).
- Daylight depth: up to ~2-2.5 x window-head height (RT; canonical: environmental-comfort.md).
- Large rooms with low ceilings feel oppressive; see environmental-comfort.md.

## A17. Reading plans
### Scales (ISO 5455)
| Scale | 1 cm on paper = | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| 1:500, 1:200 | 5 m, 2 m | Site plan |
| 1:100 | 1 m | General plans, sections, elevations |
| 1:50 | 50 cm | Furnished plans, interior design |
| 1:25, 1:20 | 25 cm, 20 cm | Kitchens, baths, closets, interior elevations |
| 1:10, 1:5, 1:1 | 10 cm, 5 cm, 1 cm | Construction and joinery details |

Graphic scale needed if printed or scaled digitally. Never scale a drawing without checking the printed scale against a known dimension.

### Dimensions
| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Partial | Between consecutive elements (wall, opening, wall) |
| Overall | Outside to outside |
| Grid | Between structural grid lines (letters and numbers in bubbles) |
| Level mark | N.P.T. (finished floor level) +0.00; N.T.N. (natural grade) |
| Units | LatAm and ES: m with 2 decimals or cm; European technical: mm. Check the drawing note |
| Window opening | Width x height and sill height |

Written dimensions govern over scaled ones.

### Common symbols
| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Thick line | Cut element (wall) |
| Thin line | Element seen below the cut plane (furniture, floor) |
| Dashed line | Element above cut plane or hidden (beams, wall cabinets, voids) |
| Plan cut plane | ~1.00-1.50 m above floor (RT, usual convention) |
| Hinged door | Leaf line + 90° arc showing swing |
| Double, swing door | Two leaves with arcs; double-acting shows arcs both sides |
| Sliding door | Leaf parallel to wall with arrow |
| Window | 2-3 thin lines in the wall; sliding with offset sashes |
| Stairs | Arrow with "up/down" (sube/baja, UP/DN) and diagonal break line |
| North arrow | Shows north; needed for orientation and sun |
| Section mark | Line with arrows and letter (A-A') showing view direction |
| Grid line | Dash-dot line with bubble, letter or number |

- Door handing: conventions differ (US viewed from outside, DIN from opening side); always confirm with the drawn arc (av per country).
- Check on plan: door swings clear of furniture, leaves not colliding, swing into hallway.

## A19. Wet core
- Group bathrooms, kitchen and laundry around few stacks: less piping, cost and leaks.
- In two-storey houses, stack bathrooms vertically.
- Keep toilet-to-stack runs short due to drain slope (≥ 1-2 %, see local code; av).
- Service shaft: 30-60 cm wide for stacks and ventilation (RT, av).
