## Spaces
Columns: size (m) | area (m²) | min side (m) | source.

### Master bedroom
| Level | Size | Area | Min side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | 2.75 x 4.20 | 11.5 | 2.75 | NDSS |
| Recommended | 3.20 x 4.00 | 12.0-13.0 | 3.00 | LHDG (12 m²); RT (size) |
| Generous | 3.80 x 4.50 | 16.0-18.0 | 3.60 | RT |

- Clearances: 2.75 m width lets a wheelchair user pass the foot of the bed with headboard on the side wall (LHDG). 75 cm clear both sides and foot of a double bed, accessible (LTH). Wardrobe front 90-105 (NEU-PZ (fs)). Dressing zone 70 x 110 (LHDG).
- Width check (RT): 70 + bed 160 + 70 = 3.00 m; 40 cm nightstands sit inside the side clearance. Length check (RT): bed 200 + aisle 90 + wardrobe 60 = 3.50 m functional minimum.
- Mistakes: judging by area only (12 m² as 2.40 x 5.00 fails a double bed with side access); door or window on the only headboard wall; ensuite or wardrobe door hitting bed or nightstand; wardrobe facing bed with < 90 cm (doors cannot open with someone standing); designing for 140 cm when the real queen/king is 160-200 cm.

### Secondary bedroom
| Level | Size | Area | Min side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | 2.55 x 4.50 | 11.5 | 2.55 | NDSS (2nd double onward) |
| Recommended | 2.75 x 4.40 | 12.0 | 2.75 | LHDG |
| Generous | 3.20 x 4.20 | 13.4 | 3.00 | RT |

- Clearances: same area must take a double or twin 90 x 200 beds (LHDG). 75 cm on one side; 75 at the foot if passing to the window (LTH). Base furniture: double bed, 2 nightstands 40 x 40, chest 45 x 75, double wardrobe 60 x 120, dressing table 50 x 105 (LHDG).
- Mistakes: width ≤ 2.40 m forces the bed against a wall (no access on one side); not testing the twin-bed option; built-in wardrobe reducing usable width below minimum (NDSS).

### Single or children's bedroom
| Level | Size | Area | Min side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | 2.15 x 3.50 | 7.5 | 2.15 | NDSS |
| Recommended | 2.50 x 3.20 | 8.0 | 2.40 | LHDG (8 m²); RT (size) |
| Generous | 3.00 x 3.50 | 10.0-10.5 | 2.80 | RT |

- Clearances: 90 x 200 bed + 40 x 40 nightstand + 45 x 75 chest + 50 x 105 desk + 60 x 60 wardrobe (LHDG). 75 cm on the free bed side (LTH). Width check (RT): bed 90 + aisle 75 + desk or wardrobe 50-60 = 2.15-2.25 m. Desk chair 75-90 behind the desk, by analogy with dining (NEU-PZ (fs)).
- Mistakes: no study desk or play area (LHDG); drawing 90 x 190 when teenagers need 90 x 200 (DEB); bunk bed under a window or radiator (RT).

### Staff bedroom
| Level | Size | Area | Min side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | 2.15 x 3.50 | 7.5 | 2.15 | NDSS (single bedroom) |
| Recommended | 2.50 x 3.20 | 8.0 | 2.40 | LHDG (single) |
| Generous | 2.80 x 3.50 + bath | 10.0 + bath | 2.60 | RT |

- No dedicated international standard; use single-bedroom values. LatAm minimums: references/codes/latam/.
- Clearances: bed 90 x 190/200, aisle 75, wardrobe 60 x 60 min, window with daylight and natural ventilation (LHDG, LTH; see environmental-comfort.md).
- Mistakes: sizing below a single bedroom (e.g. 2.0 x 2.5 m) with no direct window; access only through laundry or an unventilated kitchen; omitting wardrobe and own bathroom.

| Minimum | 1.22 x 1.22 (L: back + 1 side) | 1.5 | 1.22 | HUN |

- Clearances: hanging depth 61 comfortable, 51 min (DIM). Clear aisle 61 min, 91 rec. (HUN, DIM). Central island: 91 each side (CALC: rec. 91 aisle each side; DIM, HUN). Shoe shelf 30.5; drawer unit min depth 45.7 (DIM).
- Mistakes: 1.80 m width hanging both sides leaves a 58 cm aisle, unusable; in-swinging door blocking a hanging run; counting inside L/U corners as rod length (~60 cm lost per corner, RT); no ventilation or lighting.

### Built-in wardrobe
| Level | Size | Area | Min side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | 0.60 x 0.60 (single); 0.60 x 1.20 (double) | 0.36; 0.72 | 0.60 depth | NDSS, LHDG |
| Recommended | 0.60 x 1.80-2.40 (couple) | 1.1-1.4 | 0.60 | RT (av) |
| Generous | 0.60 x 3.00+ | 1.8+ | 0.60-0.65 | RT |

- Clearances: front clear 90-105 (hinged door + person); 60 min just to pass (NEU-PZ (fs)). Sliding doors reduce front clearance but take ~5-10 cm of usable depth (RT, av). NDSS: built-in wardrobe counts in bedroom area but cannot reduce minimum width; area above 0.72 m² (double) or 0.36 m² (single) counts as storage.
- Mistakes: depth ≤ 50 cm, hanger (≈45 cm) rubs the door (DIM: 51 min); room door swinging against the wardrobe front; rod at 1.60 under a shelf at 1.65, hangers do not fit (RT).
