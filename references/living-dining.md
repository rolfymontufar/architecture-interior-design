### Living room
| Level | Size | Area | Min side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum | 3.0 x 3.6 | 10.8 | 2.8 (2-3 person home) | LHDG (side); CALC |
| Recommended | 3.6 x 4.5 | 16.2 | 3.2 (4+ person home) | LHDG (side); CALC |
| Generous | 4.5 x 5.5 | 24.8 | 4.0 | RT |

- Derivation (CALC, typical depths, see furniture.md): sofa 85-95 + gap 40-45 + coffee table 50-60 + gap 40-45 + armchair 80-90 = 2.95-3.35 m back to back; add 90 if a walkway runs behind.
- Clearances: sofa to coffee table 40-45; main walkway 90; TV at 1.2-1.6 x diagonal (tables below).
- Mistakes: walkway cutting through the conversation area; all furniture against walls (conversation distances > 3.6 m); oversized sectional blocking doors or windows; short side < 2.8 m (sofa + table + front walkway do not fit); TV over fireplace (screen center too high, neck extension).

| Diagonal (in) | Diagonal (cm) | THX 40° (1.2 x D) | SMPTE 30° (1.6 x D) | HD 1080p (3.2 H ≈ 1.57 x D) | 4K max detail (1.6 H ≈ 0.78 x D) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 43 | 109 | 131 | 175 | 171 | 86 |
| 50 | 127 | 152 | 203 | 199 | 100 |
| 55 | 140 | 168 | 224 | 219 | 110 |
| 65 | 165 | 198 | 264 | 259 | 130 |
| 75 | 190 | 229 | 305 | 299 | 149 |
| 85 | 216 | 259 | 345 | 339 | 169 |

- Use: residential range between THX (immersive) and SMPTE (comfortable minimum). With 4K, sitting at ≥ the 4K column avoids visible pixels; farther than the HD column loses the 4K benefit (SMPTE, THX, ITU). H = 0.49 x D (16:9).
