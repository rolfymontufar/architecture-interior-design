## Key concepts
- **Visitable dwelling:** a wheelchair user can enter, move through the entry level and use a WC. Typical minimums: step-free entry, doors ≥ 80 clear, WC on entry level. Matches UK M4(1) / US "visitability". (UK-M)
- **Accessible and adaptable dwelling:** UK M4(2); adaptable to wheelchair use without major works. (UK-M)
- **Wheelchair user dwelling:** UK M4(3); ES "vivienda accesible para usuarios de silla de ruedas". (UK-M, CTE-SUA)
- **Design rule (RT):** in new dwellings, design at least the entry level with Ø 150 turn, 80 clear doors and a bathroom with lateral transfer.
