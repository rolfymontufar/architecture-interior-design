## Design principles
- **Reach:** design for P5 female (the smallest user must reach). (PH)
- **Clearance:** design for P95 male + shoes (~2.5) and clothing. (PH)
- **Adjustable:** if the P5 F to P95 M range exceeds ~10, prefer adjustable elements. (RT)
- **Population:** use data for the actual users; LatAm is ~5-12 shorter than US / Germany at P50 stature (see tables). (ACOPLA, ANSUR, GREIL)
