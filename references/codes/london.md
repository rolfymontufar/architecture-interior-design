# London: residential minimums
Codes: The London Plan 2021 (LP 2021, in force), Housing Design Standards LPG (LPG 2023, in force as guidance), Support for Housebuilding LPG (SfH LPG, March 2026, in force); Draft London Plan 2026 is NOT in force. London applies England's national rules (NDSS, Approved Documents K, M, F: see references/codes/europe/uk.md); this file covers only what London adds or changes. BP = LPG "best practice" (encouraged, not required). Clear interior dimensions in m or m² unless noted. Retrieved 2026-10-06.

## Quick reference
| Metric | London (in force) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Min. GIA | = NDSS (37-39 m² 1p ... 138 m² 6b8p), **mandatory** across London | LP 2021 D6 F(1), table 3.1 |
| Bedroom | single ≥ 7.5 m², width ≥ 2.15; double ≥ 11.5 m², 1st width ≥ 2.75, others ≥ 2.55 | LP 2021 D6 F(2-4) |
| Ceiling height | **≥ 2.50 m over ≥ 75 % of GIA** (NDSS: 2.30) | LP 2021 D6 F(8) |
| Living+dining+kitchen | 21 m² (1p), 23 (1b2p), 25 (1b3p), 27 (2b4p), 29 (3b5p), 31 (4b6p) | LPG 2023 C2.5 |
| Sitting space width | ≥ 3.0 m (≤ 2 bedspaces); ≥ 3.5 m (≥ 3) | LPG 2023 C2.6 |
| Private outdoor space | 5 m² (1-2 persons) + 1 m² per extra person; depth and width ≥ 1.50 m | LP 2021 D6 F(9); LPG C10.1 |
| Communal corridor | ≥ 1.50 m | LPG 2023 B2.1 |
| Accessibility | ≥ 10 % M4(3); rest M4(2) | LP 2021 D7 |
| Play space | ≥ 10 m² per child | LP 2021 S4 B(2); LPG B9.3 |
| New London Plan | **draft** Jul. 2026 (NOT in force); proposes 2.40 m ceiling | Draft London Plan 2026 HN8 |

## Legal status (checked 2026-10-06)
| Document | Status |
|---|---|
| **The London Plan 2021** (Spatial Development Strategy for Greater London, GLA, March 2021) | **In force.** Part of each borough's statutory development plan; planning decisions must follow it and Local Plans must be in "general conformity". Applies across all 32 boroughs and the City of London. |
| **Draft London Plan 2026** (published 27/07/2026, updated 11/09/2026) | **Draft, NOT in force.** Consultation until 15/10/2026 17:00; Examination in Public (EiP) 2027; adoption expected early 2028. Only limited weight as a material consideration (av). Does not replace the 2021 Plan. |
| **Housing Design Standards LPG** (June 2023, published 08/06/2023) | In force as guidance (not policy; expands D6). Standards "expected" (enforceable) and "best practice" (BP). Withdrawn in 2026: C4.1, B2.5, B3.1 (see below). |
| **Support for Housebuilding LPG** (March 2026; draft Nov. 2025) | In force. Temporary measures: withdraws LPG C4.1 (dual aspect) and B2.5 (8 homes per core); replaces cycle parking (LP table 10.2) until 31/03/2028. D6 still applies. |

Note: D6 and D7 apply to self-contained housing of all tenures (C3), including new build, conversion and change of use (table 3.1, note ^). D7 applies to works subject to Part M Vol. 1 (in practice, new build).

## Rooms (LP 2021 Policy D6, Housing quality and standards)
Table 3.1 is identical to NDSS table 1 (GIA and storage by bedrooms, persons, storeys): see europe/uk.md. Key difference: in London it is **mandatory** for all tenures, including conversions and changes of use; elsewhere in England only if the local plan adopts it. For > 8 bedspaces: ≈ 10 m² per extra bedspace (para. 3.6.1).

| Requirement | Value | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| GIA and built-in storage | ≥ table 3.1 (1p 39 m², 37 with shower; storage 1.0-4.0 m², includes 0.5 m² for boiler/cylinder) | D6 F(1), table 3.1 notes |
| First double bedroom | width ≥ 2.75 m (dwellings of ≥ 2 bedspaces) | D6 F(2) |
| Other doubles or twin | width ≥ 2.55 m; area ≥ 11.5 m² | D6 F(2), F(4) |
| Single bedroom | ≥ 7.5 m², width ≥ 2.15 m | D6 F(3) |
| Headroom < 1.50 m | not counted in GIA except storage (under stairs: 1 m² counts) | D6 F(5) |
| Storage 0.9-1.5 m high | counts at 50 %; < 0.9 m does not count | D6 F(6) |
| Built-in wardrobe | counts in GIA and bedroom; excess over 0.72 m² (double) or 0.36 m² (single) counts as storage; does not reduce minimum widths | D6 F(7) |
| Floor-to-ceiling | **≥ 2.50 m over ≥ 75 % of GIA**; remaining 25 % only for services in kitchen and bathroom ceilings, minimum reduction needed | D6 F(8), para. 3.6.3 |
| Private outside space | 5 m² for 1-2 persons + 1 m² per extra occupant; depth and width ≥ 1.50 m; not counted in GIA; level access; higher local standard prevails | D6 F(9), para. 3.6.9 |
| Dual aspect | maximise; normally avoid single aspect; never single aspect north-facing, with ≥ 3 bedrooms or with significant noise (para. 3.6.5) | D6 C |
| Daylight, sunlight | "sufficient and appropriate to context"; no numeric value in the Plan | D6 D |
| Waste storage | space for separate collection (paper/card, plastics, metal, glass, food, residual); no m² | D6 E |
| Play space | ≥ 10 m² per child (Policy S4 B(2)) | S4, para. 3.6.10 |
| Cycle parking (table 10.2) | 1 space (studio or 1b1p), 1.5 (1b2p), 2 (rest); visitors: 2 spaces (5-40 homes) + 1 per further 40. **Replaced until 31/03/2028** (see SfH LPG) | T5, table 10.2 |

## Differences vs national England rules
| Metric | London (LP 2021 + LPG 2023) | England (NDSS, AD M) |
|---|---|---|
| NDSS application | mandatory, all tenures, new build, conversion and change of use | optional: only if the local plan adopts it |
| Ceiling height | 2.50 m over ≥ 75 % GIA (draft 2026: 2.40) | 2.30 m over ≥ 75 % GIA |
| GIA, bedrooms, widths, storage | same as NDSS | NDSS |
| Living+dining+kitchen | 21-31 m² by occupancy (LPG C2.5) | n/r (only M4(3): 25-37 m², ADM table 3.2) |
| Living width | 3.0 / 3.5 m (LPG C2.6) | n/r |
| Private outdoor space | 5 m² + 1 m²/extra person, 1.50 x 1.50 m min. | n/r |
| Accessibility | 10 % M4(3) + 90 % M4(2) mandatory via planning | M4(1) mandatory; M4(2)/M4(3) only if local plan requires |
| Communal corridor | ≥ 1.50 m (LPG B2.1) | n/r (AD M Vol. 1 and AD B as applicable) (av) |
| Play space | ≥ 10 m² per child | n/r |
| Sound insulation | 5 dB better than AD E (LPG C5.3) | AD E |
| Water use | ≤ 105 l/person/day (LPG C7.1) | 125 l (basic requirement) or 110 l (optional) (fs) |
| Dual aspect | policy D6 C (maximise) | n/r |
| Cycle parking | 0.7-1.9 spaces/home by band (until 31/03/2028) | n/r nationally (local plans) |
