# Colombia (Bogotá): residential minimums
Codes: (1) Decreto Distrital 555 de 2021 (POT de Bogotá, in force), art. 384, regulated by Decreto Distrital 122 de 2023; (2) NSR-10 (2010, amended), Title K "Requisitos complementarios", ch. K.3 (common areas and means of egress), Ley 400 de 1997, national; (3) NTC 4140 and NTC 4145 (accessibility, corridors and stairs), referenced by NSR-10. No national or district code reviewed sets area or min side per room (bedroom, kitchen, etc.).

| Stair headroom | 2.00 m | K.3.8.3.7 |

| Egress ramps | width ≥ 1.10 m | K.3.8.6.4 |

**Stair reading:** the K.3.8.3 heading says its requirements do not apply to stairs "within apartments or individual residences"; K.3.8.3.3 does give widths: 0.90 m "inside dwellings" and 0.75 m "in single-family residential buildings with no floor limit, or in private interior stairs of apartments". Coherent reading: the specific 0.75 m rule governs inside a single dwelling; 0.90 m applies to dwelling stairs that serve as a means of egress (e.g. shared); tread 0.28 m and riser 0.18 m (K.3.8.3.4) are mandatory only on egress stairs, and good practice inside the dwelling. Interpretation, not clarified by the Advisory Commission (av). Text verified in a complete NSR-10 copy (fs).
