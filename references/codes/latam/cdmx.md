# Mexico (Mexico City, CDMX): residential minimums
Codes: Building Regulation for the Federal District (Reglamento de Construcciones para el Distrito Federal, RCDF, 2004, amended; text 04-10-2024 at PAOT) and Complementary Technical Standard for Architectural Design (Norma Técnica Complementaria para el Proyecto Arquitectónico, NTCPA), updated and published in Gaceta Oficial CDMX 12-12-2024. In force. Scope: Mexico City only; other states and municipalities have their own regulations (many take the CDMX NTC as reference). Federal accessibility (INDAABIN) is listed below under Accessibility with its own scope.
