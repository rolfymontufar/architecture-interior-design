# Chile: residential minimums
Codes: General Ordinance of Urbanism and Construction (Ordenanza General de Urbanismo y Construcciones, OGUC, DS 47 de 1992, MINVU), Title 4 ch. 1 (habitability) and ch. 2 (safety and egress); current text at BCN LeyChile (consulted 2026-10; latest amendments seen in 4.1.7: Decreto 30 VIVIENDA, D.O. 15-05-2023). Social housing: Resolución Exenta 7712/2017 (16-06-2017) "Cuadro Normativo y Tabla de Espacios y Usos Mínimos" (DS 49). National. The OGUC sets **no room areas or min sides** for general housing; only social housing (DS 49) does. Ch. 4.2 applies to common areas of collective buildings and public buildings, not dwelling interiors (art. 4.2.1).

## Accessibility
OGUC 4.1.7 (text per DS 50, D.O. 04-03-2016) applies to collective buildings and public-use buildings; it does not apply inside single-family dwellings (stairlifts are allowed for one-storey level changes inside single-family dwellings). OGUC 6.4.2: dwellings of MINVU programmes for persons with disabilities (PcD).

## Comfort
| Topic | Minimum | Ref. |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling height | 2.30 m in habitable rooms (finished work), except under beams, services and small areas under sloped roof; passages under beams 2.00 m; value set by Decreto 217 VIVIENDA, D.O. 20-02-2002 | OGUC 4.1.1 |
| Window | at least 1 window in habitable rooms; in bedrooms 1.5 m clear horizontal distance perpendicular to the window | OGUC 4.1.2 |
| Windowless bathrooms, kitchens | ventilation duct ≥ 0.16 m² | OGUC 4.1.3 |
| Thermal | max % of windows by orientation and thermal zone | OGUC 4.1.10 |

2.30 m applies since Decreto 217 (D.O. 20-02-2002), per the BCN amendment note. The 2.35 m sometimes cited would be the pre-2002 text (not seen) (av). DDU 503 (Circular Ord. 350, 11-07-2024) confirms 2.30 m for extensions and alterations of existing collective buildings (fs).

## Gaps
- Pre-2002 history (2.35 m) not seen in official text.
- No room sizes for general (non-subsidised) housing; interior doors and interior stairs n/r.
