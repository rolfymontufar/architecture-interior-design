# Costa Rica: residential minimums
Codes: INVU Building Regulation (Reglamento de Construcciones del INVU), ch. VIII "Edificaciones para uso residencial" (arts. 153 to 160 approx.); Alcance No. 62 to La Gaceta No. 54, 22-03-2018; errata Gaceta 89 (22-05-2018); amendment Alcance 145 (16-08-2018). INVU announces a "2022 update" that could not be consulted (SCIJ/SINALEVI loads text via JavaScript): numbering and values here are from the 2018 text (av). Accessibility: Ley 7600 and Regulation DE 26831-MP. Stairs and egress: Fire Department (Cuerpo de Bomberos) requirements. National, supplementary where the municipality has no own regulation (many cantons adopt their own, e.g. Goicoechea, San Carlos).

## Accessibility
Dwellings for persons with disabilities: Ley 7600 and DE 26831-MP (art. 154). Door, corridor, turning circle and ramp figures not found (av).

## Comfort
