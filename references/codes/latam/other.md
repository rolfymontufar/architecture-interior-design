# Other Latin American countries: residential minimums
Covers Uruguay (Montevideo, verified figures from a secondary copy) and candidate codes for countries without verified figures.

## Quick reference (Montevideo)
| Metric | Value | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Min dwelling | 32 m² (1 bedroom); +15 m² per bedroom | art. 3 a |
| Main room | 10 m², side 2.50 m | art. 8 a |
| Other rooms | 6.50 m² side 2.00 m, or 7.00 m² side 1.80 m | art. 8 b |
| Ceiling height | 2.40 m | art. 9 |
| Daylight | opening ≥ 1/10 of floor area | art. 10 |

## Uruguay (Montevideo)
Code: Housing habitability and hygiene conditions (Ordenanza de Higiene de la Vivienda, Intendencia de Montevideo; decree number (av)). Copy consulted (fs).
| Topic | Minimum | Ref. |
|---|---|---|
| Min dwelling | 32 m² (1 bedroom); +15 m² per bedroom: 2 bedrooms 47 m², 3 bedrooms 62 m², 4 bedrooms 78 m² | art. 3 a |
| Main room (habitación principal) | 10 m², side 2.50 m | art. 8 a |
| Other rooms | 6.50 m² with side 2.00 m, or 7.00 m² with side 1.80 m | art. 8 b |
| Ceiling height | 2.40 m; sloped ceiling: average 2.40 m, minimum 2.00 m | art. 9 |
| Direct daylight | opening ≥ 1/10 of floor area | art. 10 |
