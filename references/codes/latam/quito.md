# Ecuador (Quito): residential minimums
Codes: Technical Rules of Architecture and Urbanism (Reglas Técnicas de Arquitectura y Urbanismo, RTAU) of the Metropolitan District of Quito (DMQ), Volume 2 "Edificabilidad", v2.0, 23-10-2024 (amended 24-10-2024); Resolución ADMQ 035-2024. In force. Scope: Quito (DMQ). National: Ecuadorian Construction Standard (NEC) and INEN accessibility standards (e.g. INEN 3141 accessible bedrooms), not reviewed in detail. Cuenca and other cantons have their own rules (Cuenca: "Normas de Arquitectura y Urbanismo", PUGS annex, not reviewed).

## Accessibility
Ramps: width ≥ 0.90 m (1.20 m with turns ≥ 90°) (RTAU T2 1.3 ramps). Max slope, door, corridor, turning circle: ?; INEN standards (av).
