# Argentina (Buenos Aires City, CABA): residential minimums
Codes: CABA Building Code (Código de Edificación), Ley 6100 (Annex), sections 3.3 (rooms), 3.4 (means of egress), 3.8.1.1 (individual and collective housing); complemented by the Technical Regulations of the Enforcement Authority. Separata Boletín Oficial No. 5526, 27-12-2018 (original text; later amendments not verified) (av). Scope: CABA only; each municipality of Buenos Aires Province and other provinces has its own code. National accessibility: Decreto 914/97 (applies nationwide, included here).

## Accessibility
