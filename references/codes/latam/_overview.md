## Jurisdiction files (references/codes/latam/)
- guatemala.md: Guatemala City RG-1 (municipal) + FHA mortgage-insured housing standards (fs); no accessibility figures.
- cdmx.md: Mexico City RCDF + NTCPA 2024 (full room table); also Mexico federal accessibility (INDAABIN 2022, federal buildings).
- bogota.md: Bogotá POT Dec. 555/2021 (min dwelling only) + national NSR-10 Title K (doors, stairs); no room sizes.
- buenos-aires.md: CABA Ley 6100; also national accessibility Decreto 914/97 (applies to all of Argentina).
- chile.md: national OGUC (no room sizes for general housing) + DS 49 social housing + OGUC 4.1.7 accessibility.
- peru.md: national RNE A.010, A.020, A.120 (2023) accessibility.
- costa-rica.md: national INVU 2018 regulation (2022 update not consulted); Ley 7600.
- quito.md: Quito RTAU Vol. 2 v2.0 2024 (full room table).
- other.md: Uruguay (Montevideo) + candidate codes for El Salvador, Panama, Honduras, Nicaragua, Dominican Republic, Bolivia, Paraguay.

## Legend
- n/r not regulated in the reviewed code; ? not found; (av) to verify, not confirmed in official text; (fs) secondary source (unofficial copy, thesis or summary); RT rule of thumb; CALC calculated.
- rec. = recommended by the code itself (RG-1 art. 140 "se recomiendan").
- R = riser (contrahuella), T = tread (huella).
- FHA = Guatemala FHA standards for mortgage-insured housing (range: houses 50 to 100 m² to more than 100 m²).
- Abbreviations: GT Guatemala City; CDMX Mexico City; BOG Bogotá; CABA Buenos Aires; CL Chile; PE Peru; CR Costa Rica; UIO Quito.
