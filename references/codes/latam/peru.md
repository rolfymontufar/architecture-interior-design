# Peru: residential minimums
Codes: National Building Regulation (Reglamento Nacional de Edificaciones, RNE): Technical Standard A.010 "Condiciones generales de diseño" (RM 191-2021-VIVIENDA, El Peruano 08-07-2021); A.020 "Vivienda" (RM 188-2021-VIVIENDA, El Peruano 07-07-2021); A.120 "Accesibilidad universal en edificaciones" (RM 075-2023-VIVIENDA, El Peruano 17-02-2023). National, mandatory for all residential buildings (A.020 art. 2). In force.

## Quick reference

|---|---|---|

| Min dwelling | apartment 40.00 m²; expandable module 25.00 m²; collective 16.00 m² | A.020 art. 8.1 |
| Ceiling height | 2.30 m (bathrooms 2.10 m) | A.020 art. 9; A.010 art. 18 |
| Doors entry / bedroom / bathroom (opening) | 0.90 / 0.80 / 0.70 m; height 2.10 m | A.020 art. 12.2 |
| Interior corridor | 0.90 m | A.020 art. 13 |

| Riser / tread | ≤ 0.18 m / ≥ 0.25 m | A.010 art. 23.2 |
| Daylight / ventilation | openings ≥ 10% of floor, ≥ 5% opening to exterior | A.020 art. 12.4 |
| Accessible door (common areas) | main 1.00 m; interior 0.90 m | A.120 art. 28 b |
| Accessible ramp | 10% up to 0.30 m; 8% from 0.31 to 0.72 m | A.120 art. 6 |
| Elevator | when 5th floor is > 12.00 m above entrance | A.020 art. 16 |

## Circulation
| Element | Minimum | Ref. |
|---|---|---|
| Door openings (vanos) | main entrance 0.90 m; bedroom, living, kitchen, dining 0.80 m; bathrooms 0.70 m; entrance to multifamily 1.20 m; height 2.10 m | A.020 art. 12.2 (Cuadro 06) |
| Corridors (pasajes) | inside dwelling 0.90 m; access to up to 2 dwellings 1.00 m; up to 4 dwellings 1.20 m; common areas 1.20 m | A.020 art. 13; A.010 art. 20 |
| Interior stair | width 0.90 m between walls; 0.80 m with one side open; spiral or winder with tread ≥ 0.25 m at 0.50 m from axis; handrail at 1.00 m on one side | A.020 art. 15.1 |
| Integrated multifamily stair | width 1.20 m between walls | A.020 art. 15.2 |
| Tread (paso) | ≥ 0.25 m in dwellings | A.010 art. 23.2 b |
| Riser (contrapaso) | ≤ 0.18 m | A.010 art. 23.2 c |
| Flights and landings | max 17 steps between landings; landing ≥ 0.90 m in straight stairs | A.010 art. 23.2 a |
| Ramps (general) | width ≥ 1.00 m; max slope 12% (unless the specific standard says otherwise) | A.010 art. 21 |

A.010 art. 21 (general ramps 12%) applies outside the accessible route; in residential common areas A.120 art. 6 governs. An MVCS presentation (Dirección de Accesibilidad) confirms the ramp values.

## Accessibility
**A.120 (RM 075-2023-VIVIENDA).** Mandatory in buildings serving the public and in common circulation areas of residential buildings (art. 2); it does not regulate the interior of each dwelling.
| Topic | Minimum | Ref. A.120 |
|---|---|---|
| Entry doors | opening ≥ 1.00 m; between 2 consecutive swing doors 1.20 m clear | art. 4 b, d |
| Doors in residential common areas | main 1.00 m; interior 0.90 m; lever handle, lock ≤ 1.20 m | art. 28 b, e |
| Corridor, accessible route | width ≥ 0.90 m (by occupancy); height 2.10 m; turning 1.50 x 1.50 m every 25 m (residential common areas); projections ≤ 0.15 m | art. 5 f, j, k; art. 28 c |
| Level changes | up to 0.006 m free; 0.006 to 0.013 m bevelled; > 0.013 m ramp | art. 5 c |
| Ramps | width ≥ 1.00 m incl. handrails; max slope 10% up to 0.30 m, 8% from 0.31 to 0.72 m; max run 9.00 m + landing 1.50 m; turning Ø 1.20 m (occupancy < 500) or 1.50 x 1.50 m (> 500); cross slope < 2% | art. 6 a to e |
| Ramp handrails | double: 0.85 to 0.90 m and 0.25 m below; Ø 0.04 to 0.05 m; extension 0.20 m | art. 7 a to c |
| Guardrails | level change > 0.55 m: ≥ 1.00 m; kickplate 0.15 m | art. 7 d |
| Under stairs | areas < 2.10 m high protected at 0.85 to 0.90 m | art. 6 i |
| Residential elevator | car ≥ 1.00 x 1.25 m (public use 1.20 x 1.40 m) | art. 8 a, b |
| Accessible WC | turning Ø 1.50 m; door opening 1.00 m; WC cubicle 1.50 x 2.00 m; lateral transfer 0.80 x 1.20 m; seat 0.45 to 0.50 m; WC axis 0.40 m from wall; grab bars at 0.75 m (to axis), 0.25 m above seat lid | arts. 13.1 a, b; 15 a, d, e |
| Accessible basin | rim at 0.85 m; clear below 0.75 m; front space 0.80 x 1.20 m | art. 14 d |
| Accessible shower | 0.90 x 1.20 m without curb; adjacent space 1.50 x 1.50 m; seat 0.45 to 0.50 m; taps at 1.20 m | art. 17 |
| Residential common access | ramps or mechanical means in entrance lobbies and in buildings requiring an elevator | art. 28 a |

**Previous version 2019 (RM 072-2019-VIVIENDA), superseded:** main doors 1.20 m and interior 0.90 m (art. 4 b); slopes 12% (≤ 0.25 m) to 2% (> 2.01 m) (art. 6 b); art. 30 "Unidades de vivienda" (interior doors 0.90 m, turning Ø 1.50 m in all rooms), which does not appear in the 2023 version.
