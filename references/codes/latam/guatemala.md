# Guatemala (Guatemala City): residential minimums
Codes: RG-1 Plan Regulador, Building Regulation of Guatemala City (Reglamento de Construcción de la Ciudad de Guatemala, Municipalidad de Guatemala), title "Normas mínimas de diseño", ch. II to IV, arts. 128 to 148. Municipal only (other municipalities have their own; the POT 2008 regulates zoning and indices, not room sizes). Compiled text with amendments to 2002 to 2004, base version from the 1960s (av). FHA Planning and Construction Standards (mortgage-insured housing) (fs): FHA also requires the municipal code; the stricter one governs.

**Hallway conflict, arts. 140 vs 144:** art. 140 i is a recommendation for dwelling rooms (0.90 m); art. 144 is mandatory ("nunca menor de un metro") and refers to corridors "of a building". Prudent reading: 1.00 m in common and egress corridors, 0.90 m inside the dwelling; if the Office does not accept this reading, use 1.00 m (interpretation, not confirmed by the Municipality) (av). The muniguate.com PDF did not respond in the last iteration.

## Accessibility
- Law on Care for Persons with Disabilities (Decreto 135-96) and CONADI. No NTG/COGUANOR technical norm with figures (door, ramp) applicable to housing was found, and CONADI's "Manual Técnico de Accesibilidad Universal" was not obtained (av).
- Door, corridor, ramp, turning circle, accessible bathroom size, grab bars, WC seat, basin: all not verified.
