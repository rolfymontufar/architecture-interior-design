# United Kingdom (England): residential minimums
Codes: Technical housing standards: Nationally Described Space Standard (NDSS), DCLG, 27/03/2015 (notes 19/05/2016); Approved Document K (protection from falling, collision and impact), 2013 edition; Approved Document M Vol. 1 (dwellings), 2015 edition incorporating 2016 amendments; Approved Document F Vol. 1 (ventilation, dwellings), 2026 edition. England only (Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland have their own rules). NDSS is optional: applies only where the local plan adopts it. AD K and M4(1) mandatory (Building Regulations 2010); M4(2) and M4(3) are optional requirements triggered by a planning condition. London adds its own rules: see references/codes/london.md.

## Accessibility
M4(1) mandatory; M4(2) (accessible and adaptable) and M4(3) (wheelchair user) only where the local plan requires them. Dimensions in Rooms and Circulation above. M4(3) wheelchair storage and transfer: 1100 x 1700 mm on entrance storey, reached from a space ≥ 1200 mm (AD M, 3.25).
