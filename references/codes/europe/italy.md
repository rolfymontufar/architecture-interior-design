# Italy: residential minimums
Codes: D.M. Sanità 5 luglio 1975 (requisiti igienico-sanitari dei locali d'abitazione), arts. 1-7; D.M. LL.PP. 14 giugno 1989, n. 236 (barriere architettoniche); D.L. 69/2024 "Salva Casa" (conv. L. 105/2024): exceptions to regularise existing dwellings. National, in force; municipal Regolamenti edilizi may be stricter.

## Quick reference
| Metric | Value | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Single bedroom | ≥ 9 m² | DM 1975 art. 2 |
| Double bedroom | ≥ 14 m² | DM 1975 art. 2 |
| Living room | ≥ 14 m² | DM 1975 art. 2 |
| Studio (monolocale) | ≥ 28 m² (1 person), ≥ 38 m² (2) | DM 1975 art. 3 |
| Area per inhabitant | 14 m² (first 4), 10 m² each extra | DM 1975 art. 2 |
| Ceiling height | 2.70 (2.40 corridors, bathrooms, storerooms) | DM 1975 art. 1 |
| Doors | entrance 0.80; interior 0.75 | DM 236/1989 8.1.1 (fs) |
| Corridor | ≥ 1.00 | DM 236/1989 8.1.9 (fs) |
| Daylight | mean FLD ≥ 2 %; openable ≥ 1/8 floor | DM 1975 art. 5 |
| Internal stair | n/r nationally | n/a |
