# Spain (national, CTE): residential minimums
Codes: Código Técnico de la Edificación (CTE), Real Decreto 314/2006, consolidated with RD 732/2019 and RD 450/2022; DB-SUA (safety of use and accessibility) and DB-HS 3 (indoor air quality). National, new build and interventions, in force. Sets NO room or dwelling sizes (regions and municipalities do: see madrid.md, catalonia.md). DB-HE (energy, HE0 to HE6): no room dimensions.

|---|---|---|

| Headroom (paso, not ceiling height) | ≥ 2.10 m restricted use; ≥ 2.20 m elsewhere; door ≥ 2.00 m | SUA 2, 1.1.1 |
| Private stair (uso restringido) | width ≥ 0.80; riser ≤ 0.20; tread ≥ 0.22 | SUA 1, 4.1.1, 4.1.2 |
| Guarding | ≥ 0.90 m (drop ≤ 6 m); ≥ 1.10 m (> 6 m) | SUA 1, 4.1.4, 3.2.1 |

| Turning circle (accessible) | Ø 1.50 | SUA Anejo A |
| Ramp max. | 12 %; accessible 10 % (< 3 m), 8 % (< 6 m), 6 % rest | SUA 1, 4.3.1 |
| Ventilation, wet room | 6 / 7 / 8 l/s (0-1 / 2 / ≥ 3 bedrooms) | HS 3, table 2.1 |
| Cooker extract | ≥ 50 l/s | HS 3, 2.1.4 |

## Accessibility
Wheelchair-accessible dwelling (vivienda accesible para usuarios de silla de ruedas), SUA Anejo A; number of such dwellings per applicable regulation (SUA 9, 1.2.1).

| Space | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Hallways | clear ≥ 1.10 m; pinch points ≥ 1.00 m over ≤ 0.50 m |
| Entrance hall | turning Ø 1.50 m |
| Doors | ≥ 0.80 m clear (≥ 0.78 m with leaf); Ø 1.20 m clear both sides; handle at 0.80-1.20 m |
| Main living room | turning Ø 1.50 m with furniture |
| All bedrooms | turning Ø 1.50 m; 0.90 m on one side of the bed and 0.90 m at the foot |
| Kitchen | turning Ø 1.50 m; worktop ≤ 85 cm; clear space under sink and hob 70 x 80 x 60 cm |
| Bathroom (at least one) | turning Ø 1.50 m; conditions of appendix C |

## Comfort
Ceiling height: n/r (2.10 m is headroom for passage, not a ceiling height). Daylight: CTE sets no %.

DB-HS 3, table 2.1, minimum ventilation rates (l/s):
| Dwelling | Main bedroom | Other bedrooms | Living and dining | Wet rooms: total min. | Per wet room |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 or 1 bedroom | 8 | n/a | 6 | 12 | 6 |
| 2 bedrooms | 8 | 4 | 8 | 24 | 7 |
| 3 or more bedrooms | 8 | 4 | 10 | 33 | 8 |

Also: 1.5 l/s per habitable room when unoccupied (HS 3, 2.1.1); cooking-zone extract ≥ 50 l/s (HS 3, 2.1.4); kitchens, dining rooms, bedrooms and living rooms need an openable window or external door (HS 3, 3.1.1).
