# Madrid (City), Spain: residential minimums
Codes: Normas Urbanísticas of the Plan General de Ordenación Urbana de Madrid 1997 (PGOUM), art. 7.3.4 (dwelling programme) with 7.3.3 and 7.3.5; amendment (Modificación de las NNUU, MPG) finally approved 2023 (BOCM 14/11/2023, in force November 2023). Municipal (city of Madrid). Stairs, ventilation, accessibility: CTE (see spain-cte.md).
Scope note: the Comunidad de Madrid has no general habitability decree for open-market housing (av); without municipal regulation the Orden del Ministerio de la Gobernación of 29/02/1944 (BOE 01/03/1944) applies, per the City itself (Consulta Urbanística CU 10/2025). Projects applied for before Nov. 2023 follow the old text + Orden 1944 (CU 10/2025).
