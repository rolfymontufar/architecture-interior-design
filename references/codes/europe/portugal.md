# Portugal: residential minimums
Codes: Regulamento Geral das Edificações Urbanas (RGEU), Decreto-Lei n.º 38382/1951, as worded by DL 650/75 (arts. 46, 65-71, 84, 87). Accessibility: DL 163/2006 (technical rules). Licensing reform (DL 10/2024) and future Código da Construção (av). National, new build, in force. The tables of arts. 66 and 67 are images in DL 650/75 ("ver documento original"): those values are (fs). Typology T0-T6 = number of bedrooms.

| Bathrooms | T0-T2 ≥ 3.5 m²; T3-T4 ≥ 4.5 m² in 2 spaces; T5+ ≥ 6 m² in 2 spaces | RGEU art. 68 |

## Circulation
| Item | Value | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Corridors | ≥ 1.10 m; secondary ≤ 1.50 m long: ≥ 0.90 m | RGEU art. 70 |
| Doors | ≥ 0.77 m clear (útil) | DL 163/2006, sec. 3.3 (fs) |
| Stairs | single-family ≥ 0.80 m; collective ≤ 2 floors or 4 dwellings ≥ 0.90 m; larger ≥ 1.10 m (between walls 1.10 / 1.20 m) | RGEU art. 46.1-4 |
| Steps (collective) | tread ≥ 0.25 m, riser ≤ 0.193 m; 3-5 floors without lift: ≥ 0.28 / ≤ 0.175 m | RGEU art. 46.7 |

## Accessibility
In-dwelling (DL 163/2006, sec. 3.3 (fs)): corridors ≥ 1.10 m (0.90 m if ≤ 1.50 m without side doors); doors ≥ 0.77 m clear; 360° turning in entrance and kitchen; ≥ 1.20 m between worktops.

## Comfort
| Item | Value | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling height | floor to floor ≥ 2.70 m; clear ≥ 2.40 m; 2.20 m in halls, corridors, bathrooms, pantries; sloped ceilings: min. over ≥ 80 % | RGEU art. 65 |
| Windows | ≥ 1/10 of the room area, min. 1.08 m² (gross) | RGEU art. 71 |
| Bathroom window | ≥ 0.54 m², openable ≥ 0.36 m² (or effective ventilation) | RGEU art. 87 |
